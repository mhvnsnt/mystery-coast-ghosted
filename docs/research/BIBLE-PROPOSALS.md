# Bible proposals (NOT canon)

Clearly-marked proposals for SHOW-BIBLE.md, from the 2026-10-16 research.
These are IDEAS for the owner to approve, reject, or change. Nothing here
rewrites the bible. The bible is owner canon; he decides.

## Proposal 1: lock the episode formula

The bible says the cast "just so happen to solve [mysteries] sometimes,
mostly by accident." Proposal: adopt the 6-beat mystery formula as the
show's engine (see SCOOBY-DOO-1969-PIPELINE.md), with deliberate lazy-cast
subversions written into the bible: the trap ALWAYS backfires, the reveal
happens too early, nobody cares about the unmasking. The formula does the
structural work so the comedy can be lazy.

## Proposal 2: cold-open chaos + consequence-free reset

Proposal: every episode cold-opens with chaos unrelated to the plot (the
ATHF Dr. Weird slot), and endings reset everything with no continuity.
This is structural permission for the cast to stay lazy: no arcs, no
consequences, no growth. The bible's "bullshitting around" becomes a
format rule, not just a vibe.

## Proposal 3: the decoy premise, stated in the bible

ATHF sold executives "they're detectives" and abandoned it on camera with
an in-universe excuse ("that wasn't making us a whole lot of money").
Proposal: the bible explicitly names the decoy ("they solve mysteries")
and the in-universe excuse for abandoning it, so every episode can play
the tension between the premise and the cast's laziness.

## Proposal 4: one-bar greenlight

Proposal: the bible names the single decider whose laugh is the only note
(SGC2C model). No comedy by committee. This is a production rule with
creative consequences: faster episodes, one consistent voice.

## Proposal 5: the straight-man rule

Proposal: the bible requires at least one sincere party per episode (a
guest, a victim, a local authority) for the idiots to play against. SGC2C
proved the format collapses when everyone performs. Protect the asymmetry
in casting and writing.

## Proposal 6: voice-first production as canon

Proposal: the bible states the production order (voices before animation,
animatic before animation) so writers and performers know the voice
session IS the performance. Catchphrases that emerge from improv get
canonized into the bible (the "Jinkies" method).

## Proposal 7: design-for-pipeline character rules

Proposal: the bible (or a character design bible) requires every
character design to include: separated head/body/limb levels, clean joint
separation points, seam-hiding accessories, a limited mouth shape set.
Character design is a production decision, per the HB method.
